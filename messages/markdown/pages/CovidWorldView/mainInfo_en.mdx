This view provides a comprehensive interactive visualization of historical COVID-19 infection data worldwide spanning 2020 to 2022, stratified at country and subregional levels. When analyzing international trends, country-specific factors must be taken into account: these include reporting delays during weekends and public holidays, disparities in national healthcare and testing capacities, as well as evolving criteria for recording infections and deaths over time. The data used and further information on data collection can be found in the [Google COVID-19 Open Data repository](https://health.google.com/covid-19/open-data/raw-data) and the [Robert Koch Institute (RKI) SARS-CoV-2 Infections in Germany repository](https://github.com/robert-koch-institut/SARS-CoV-2-Infektionen_in_Deutschland).
