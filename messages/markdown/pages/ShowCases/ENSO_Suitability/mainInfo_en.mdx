This view demonstrates predictions of mosquito habitat suitability up to 6 months into the future based on future climate scenarios (such as SEAS5 forecasts). It features a side-by-side comparison of a **Forecast Map** and a **Reference Map**. Above the maps, linecharts display the development over time for 4 key climatic factors (**Temperature**, **Dewpoint Temperature**, **Wind Speed**, and **Total Precipitation**) comparing the reference period with the forecast, accompanied by bar charts showing mean deltas.
