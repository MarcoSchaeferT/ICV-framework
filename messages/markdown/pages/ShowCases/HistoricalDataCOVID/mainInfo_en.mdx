This view presents aggregated historical COVID-19 infection data in Germany, stratified by state. When analyzing temporal trends, account for two primary artifacts: short-term reporting delays during weekends and public holidays, and macro-level distortions caused by shifting national testing strategies and capacities throughout the pandemic. Further methodological documentation and the foundational raw data used for aggregation are available in the [Robert Koch Institute (RKI) SARS-CoV-2 Infections in Germany repository](https://github.com/robert-koch-institut/SARS-CoV-2-Infektionen_in_Deutschland).
